# Birthday answer key, draft

Drafted September 25, 2026 for Nathan to check. For each of 30 dates, the people a well read American would expect near the top of that date's page.

- **Must**: belongs in the top 10 of the date page. Missing one is a failure.
- **Should**: expected, but it is fine if a few fall just outside the top 10.

Every birth date here was checked against Wikidata on September 25, 2026, through each person's English Wikipedia page, not by name. A note in brackets shows where the live site stands today: "missing" means the person is not in the database at all, and "now #15" means they are on the page but ranked 15th. No note means they are already in the top 10.

How to check it: cross out anyone who does not belong, and add anyone obvious who is missing. Keep the "Must" lines short. They are the ones the score counts.

## One date bug found while checking

Leonardo da Vinci and Michelangelo are recorded in the old Julian calendar, the one Europe used before 1582. The Wikidata query service converts every date to the modern calendar before it answers, so Leonardo comes back as April 24, 1452 instead of April 15, and Michelangelo as March 15 instead of March 6. Every book and every other birthday site uses April 15 and March 6. The importer has to read the calendar a date was written in and keep the date as written. It does not do that yet.
