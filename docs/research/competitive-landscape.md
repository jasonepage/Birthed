# Competitive Landscape Research

Research date: September 4, 2026. Every app metric below came from Apple's public iTunes lookup interface, which returns structured release and rating fields. Rendered App Store web pages gave wrong dates on two apps during this research, so only the structured figures are trusted here.

Each claim is labeled. **Verified** means a page was read directly. **Inferred** means a conclusion drawn from verified facts. **Unverified** means it could not be confirmed.

---

## 1. Birthday freebie and deal apps

Three iOS apps exist in this category. All are small.

| App | Developer | Released | Last update | Rating | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Birthday Hunter: Free Rewards](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/birthday-hunter-free-rewards/id6739016081) | Kpoga Software LLC | 2025-02-18 | 2026-09-02 (v9.0.0) | 3.95 stars, 21 ratings | Actively maintained. Free with in app purchases from $0.99 to $99.99 |
| [Freebird: Birthday Freebies](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/freebird-birthday-freebies/id6776373560) | Qupid, LLC | 2026-06-09 | 2026-09-02 (v1.0.8) | 5.0 stars, 22 ratings | Newest, ad supported, listed under Finance. Also on Google Play |
| [bdayfreeday](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bdayfreeday-1-birthday-app/id977371455) | Green Trail Technologies | 2015-04-11 | 2026-04-07 (v4.0.0) | 1.82 stars, 22 ratings | Local business map model. Reviews say signup is broken |

**Verified:** the entire category holds roughly 65 combined ratings. That is not a defended market.

**Verified:** Freebird's App Store description is the closest anyone comes to the qualification thesis. It reads: "Most birthday rewards make you sign up weeks ahead. Freebird turns 'your birthday is coming' into a simple checklist: see every reward you qualify for, sorted by how soon you need to claim it."

**Inferred:** Freebird appears to sort by signup deadline only. Its listing never claims to model prior purchase requirements, loyalty app requirements, or location restrictions. This needs a hands on install to confirm, and it is the single most important unknown in this research, because it determines whether the core differentiator is already taken.

### Websites that dominate the search results

**Verified** as top organic results across four separate searches:

- [heyitsfree.net/birthday-freebies](https://www.heyitsfree.net/birthday-freebies/) is the most structured competitor found anywhere. Searchable table, 300 plus companies, taxonomy fields for freebie type, perks, food class and locations, a "Details and Proof" column, and state filtering.
- [thefreebieguy.com/birthday-freebies](https://thefreebieguy.com/birthday-freebies/) lists 150 plus in a two column table. Records rules inconsistently. The Starbucks entry says "you must have made at least one Star-earning transaction" and the Sephora entry says "Free in store. Spend $25+ to redeem online," but most entries omit signup lead time and dine in restrictions entirely.
- [thepennyhoarder.com](https://www.thepennyhoarder.com/save-money/birthday-freebies/) lists 115.
- [daily.slickdeals.net](https://daily.slickdeals.net/shopping/205-businesses-that-will-give-you-a-birthday-freebie-this-year/) lists 205.
- [cozymeal.com](https://www.cozymeal.com/magazine/free-birthday-food) lists 76.
- Others: [passionatepennypincher.com](https://passionatepennypincher.com/birthday-freebies/), [choosefi.com](https://choosefi.com/frugal-living/birthday-freebies), [griproom.com](https://www.griproom.com/fun/ultimate-list-of-birthday-freebies-and-deals-2025-150-places-to-get-free-stuff-on-your-birthday), [birthdayhunter.com](https://www.birthdayhunter.com/).

**Verified:** no app tracks qualification rules. The best structured source anywhere is heyitsfree.net, which is a website, and its fields are category tags rather than per offer eligibility logic. This is a real gap.

## 3. Famous birthdays and birthday twins

**famousbirthdays.com**, per [Similarweb data for July 2026](https://www.similarweb.com/website/famousbirthdays.com/): 23.4 million monthly visits, ranked 2,370 globally, 77.61 percent of traffic from organic search, top age bracket 25 to 34, bounce rate 64.32 percent, 2.91 pages per visit, average duration 1 minute 18 seconds.

Business model per [Digiday, January 2020](https://digiday.com/media/famous-birthdays-building-growing-media-company-back-programmatic-ads/): pure programmatic advertising, over 500 million impressions per month, roughly 35 staff, bootstrapped and profitable, with a deliberate decision to have no direct sales team. Note that the traffic figure is current and the financial reporting is six years old.

Its [iOS app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/famous-birthdays/id646707938) is the category giant at 4.80 stars and 23,663 ratings, updated 2026-08-27.

**Born Today: Daily Fame** ([id6755918680](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6755918680)): developer Mohamed El Bouhssini, released 2025-12-03, still version 1.0, last updated 2025-12-04, 0 ratings, Lifestyle category, free and ad supported, 9.8 MB, iOS 17 and up. Listed features include a daily spotlight with biography and quotes, an archive of famous birthdays and historical events, a Birthday Twin finder, favorites, share cards, Live Activities, widgets, local notifications, and themes.

**Inferred:** shipped once and abandoned. Nine months without an update and zero displayed ratings.

Shared birthday social apps, both essentially pre traction:

- [Twinly, Birthday And Party](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/twinly-birthday-and-party/id6756940397): released 2026-01-15, updated 2026-06-09, 1 rating. Genuinely social with birthday rooms, instant matching, group chats, a feed, streaks and direct messages. Premium at $1.99.
- [Twiny, Find your twins](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/twiny-find-your-twins/id6449654342): updated 2026-08-23, 1 rating at 1.0 stars.

## 4. Adjacent threats

**Unverified negative:** no evidence was found that Fetch, Ibotta, Rakuten or Honey surface birthday offers as a feature. Comparison coverage was searched ([Firstcard](https://www.firstcard.app/learn/fetch-vs-ibotta-vs-rakuten), [FinanceBuzz](https://financebuzz.com/fetch-rewards-vs-ibotta), [Fetch's own blog](https://fetch.com/blog/smart-shopping/best-online-shopping-rewards-apps-top-picks)) and birthday never appears as a surface. This is absence of evidence, not proof of absence. Their in app surfaces and full help centers were not read.

**Verified:** DoorDash runs search landing pages such as [doordash.com/near-me/category/birthday-deals](https://www.doordash.com/near-me/category/birthday-deals), but these are merchant browse marketing pages, not offer tracking.

**Verified:** no birthday offer feature found in Apple Wallet. Google Calendar [manages birthday events only](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/13748346) with no offers layer.

## 5. What people actually complain about

### The offers themselves are getting worse

**Verified,** [Axios, July 2023](https://www.axios.com/2023/07/10/birthday-freebies-starbucks-dunkin-sephora): Sephora added a $25 minimum for online redemption, Dunkin' eliminated its free birthday beverage, and Red Robin made the birthday burger dine in only with a $4.99 minimum. A retail expert quoted in the piece says rewards are "definitely becoming stingier and more restrictive, which is not something that always goes down well."

### Complaints about existing freebie apps

**Verified** App Store reviews:

- Birthday Hunter, 1 star: "Nothing but ads, surveys and junk coming to your email. Not one birthday reward found. AVOID!"
- Birthday Hunter, 1 star, dated 08/07/2025: "There are ads that pop up way too frequently... I ended up getting so frustrated with it that I just deleted it and asked ChatGPT."
- Birthday Hunter, 5 stars but qualified: "The ads are too much but worth it."
- bdayfreeday: "I can't even sign up through email or facebook to use the app" and "Today is my birthday and the app does not show any freebies."

The second review matters more than the rest. Users are already routing around these apps to a large language model. That names the real competitor.

### Complaints about reminder apps

**Verified** from [hip reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hip-birthday-reminder-app/id401949944?see-all=reviews), though these are older:

- 3 stars: "The reminders work about half the time... No phone support so you're at the mercy of email."
- 2 stars: "5 contacts in and I've already reached the pre-upgrade limit... $20 or a monthly subscription."
- 4 stars: reminders fire an hour off in January, February, November and December. That is a daylight saving time bug, and it is a direct warning for our own notification scheduling.

## Gaps that could not be filled

1. **Reddit was completely inaccessible** in the research environment. Both reddit.com and old.reddit.com returned a site block, the JavaScript Object Notation endpoint was blocked, and the search index filtered reddit.com out of results. No r/frugal, r/freebies or r/personalfinance complaint threads could be read. This is the single biggest hole and should be filled from an unblocked browser.
2. **Download and revenue figures** for every app named. Ratings counts are real. Installs and revenue are not public.
3. **Whether Fetch, Ibotta, Rakuten or Honey have birthday features.** Searched, nothing found, treat as unverified.
4. **Freebird's actual in app data model.** Whether it stores prior purchase and loyalty requirements per offer, or only deadlines.
5. **Born Today's real install base.** Apple returns 0 ratings, which means insufficient for display, not necessarily zero users.
6. **Famous Birthdays' current revenue.** Traffic is current, financial reporting is from 2020.
7. **bdayfreeday's 2026 relaunch quality.** It shipped version 4.0.0 in April 2026, but the reviews read were from 2015 to 2017.
