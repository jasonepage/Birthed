# Birthday Reward Terms Research

Research date: September 4, 2026. Primary sources are the brand's own terms or rewards pages. Secondary sources are labeled as such and carry lower confidence.

**This file exists to prove one point:** birthday reward qualification is genuinely complicated, the published lists are genuinely wrong, and the rules change often enough that any stored record needs an expiration date. Section B is the input to the database schema.

## B. Rule types observed

This is the schema input. Every distinct category of qualification rule actually seen in the sources above.

1. Loyalty account required. Universal across all verified brands.
2. Channel of enrollment requirement. App only (Dutch Bros) versus web or app (IHOP, Red Robin) versus account or email.
3. Separate marketing consent requirement. Jersey Mike's requires opting into the email program, which is distinct from having an account.
4. Birth date on file requirement, separate from having an account.
5. Advance signup deadline, fixed number of days. Starbucks 7, IHOP 7.
6. Advance signup deadline, relative period. Ulta requires it before the birthday month. Jersey Mike's and Red Robin say simply before the birthday or at registration.
7. Prior qualifying purchase with a lookback window. Jersey Mike's uses a 12 month lookback **and adds a product category constraint**, since the order must contain a regular or giant sub, wrap, or sub bowl.
8. Prior qualifying activity that recurs annually. Starbucks requires a star earning transaction each year, and Jersey Mike's must be satisfied every year.
9. Tier dependent redemption window. Starbucks Green 1 day, Gold 7 days, Reserve 30 days.
10. Tier dependent reward content. Sephora, Panera, Starbucks.
11. Redemption window types. Birthday date only, N days from birthday, **N days from reward collection** (Dutch Bros, anchored to collection rather than to the birthday), calendar birthday month, and month start activation (Red Robin).
12. Reward as currency versus reward as item. Jersey Mike's grants 72 points and IHOP grants 5 PanCoins, so the **point expiry clock** becomes the real deadline. IHOP is 365 days after 07/22/2025 and was 730 days before it.
13. Minimum spend to redeem, which is channel specific. Sephora is $25 online and $0 in store. Ulta requires a subtotal above $0. Red Robin's kids offer requires $4.99.
14. Basket composition requirement. Ulta requires at least one non Marketplace product in the bag.
15. Channel exclusion. Ulta excludes Marketplace, third party marketplaces and auto replenish.
16. Geographic exclusion. Ulta excludes purchases outside the United States.
17. Participating locations only, or partial franchise acceptance. IHOP says participating restaurants. Baskin-Robbins says most shops but not all.
18. Dine in only, or presence of the beneficiary required. Red Robin's kids offer requires the child to be physically present.
19. Age eligibility. Baskin-Robbins requires 13 and up. Red Robin's kids offer is for 17 and under.
20. Item category exclusions inside the reward. Starbucks excludes multi serve items, alcohol, merchandise and packaged coffee.
21. Inventory contingent availability. Sephora and Ulta both say while supplies last, with no substitutes or rainchecks.
22. Single use, or once per year.
23. Non transferable or non shareable. Note that Dutch Bros distinguishes: points rewards are shareable, birthday rewards are not.
24. Anti resale clause. Sephora states that reselling the gift violates the terms.
25. No cash or gift card value.
26. Cannot combine with other rewards or discounts.
27. Redemption method requirement. Starbucks requires scanning the member barcode in the app or presenting a registered card. Ulta in store requires a code from the app or the birthday email.
28. Add on family or dependent enrollment. Baskin-Robbins allows up to 5 additional family members.
29. Sunset or grandfather clause with a cutoff date. Red Robin.
30. Unilateral change without notice clause. See section C.

### What this means for the schema

- The redemption window needs an **anchor** field (birthday date, birthday month, reward collection date, month start), not just a duration.
- The advance signup deadline needs both a fixed days form and a relative period form.
- The prior purchase rule needs a window, a product category filter, and a flag for whether it recurs annually.
- Minimum spend and location validity both need to be **per channel**, not per offer.

## C. Staleness evidence

- **Red Robin is actively killing its birthday burger.** Terms versioned 06/01/2026 state the offer "will not be renewed" after a final 2026 redemption. Nearly every listicle still advertises it.
- **Chipotle relaunched Rewards on 04/13/2026** and changed the birthday reward to a choice of four items. Its own terms, updated the same day, do not mention the birthday reward at all, so the binding rules live only in marketing copy. That is an offer with no authoritative terms.
- **Buffalo Wild Wings' terms, updated June 2026, and its FAQ contain no birthday reward,** despite widespread listicle claims. Either discontinued or never contractually documented.
- **Denny's rewards terms, effective 03/13/2025, contain no birthday reward section,** though the FAQ promises birthday gifts.
- **Panera overhauled MyPanera** with a new paid tier, apparently changing the birthday reward from a bakery treat to a You Pick Two for paying members.
- **IHOP silently shortened PanCoin expiry** from 730 to 365 days effective 07/22/2025, directly shortening the usable life of the birthday grant.
- **Change without notice language is explicit** at Jersey Mike's ("at any time, without notice to you"), Dutch Bros ("with or without notice... even though such changes may affect the value of points or rewards already accumulated"), Sephora, Ulta and Starbucks.
- Effective dates cluster tightly: Starbucks March 2026, Chipotle April 2026, Red Robin June 2026, Buffalo Wild Wings June 2026. That implies a revision cadence of roughly a year or faster.

**Conclusion for the product:** every offer record must carry a `terms_last_verified` date and a `source_url`, and any record older than roughly six months should be treated as suspect and shown to the user as unverified.

## D. Could not verify

- **Firehouse Subs.** All fields. Both terms pages render client side and returned no content.
- **Texas Roadhouse.** All fields. The VIP Club signup page collects a birthday but describes no benefit, and the loyalty terms page renders client side. **Do not assume a free appetizer exists.**
- **Denny's.** Reward item, app requirement, advance signup, prior purchase, redemption window, location exclusions, minimum purchase.
- **Panera.** Everything except the secondary tier description. No official page confirmed a birthday reward exists at all.
- **Buffalo Wild Wings.** Whether a birthday reward exists outside the published terms.
- **Chipotle.** Account and app requirements, advance signup deadline, prior purchase requirement, location exclusions, minimum purchase, single use, transferability, plus the unresolved 30 day versus birthday month conflict.
- **Starbucks.** Location exclusions for licensed stores, airports and grocery.
- **Sephora.** Advance enrollment deadline. The terms state none.
- **Jersey Mike's.** Whether the 72 points are usable immediately, and any location exclusions.
- **Dutch Bros.** Location exclusions, and whether an advance birthday entry rule exists beyond the same day case.
- **Baskin-Robbins.** Coupon expiration and advance signup deadline.

Out of 14 brands investigated, **9 were substantially verified, 3 could not be verified at all, and 2 appear to have no birthday reward despite being widely listed as having one.** That failure rate on a hand picked list of well known brands is the strongest argument for the product.
