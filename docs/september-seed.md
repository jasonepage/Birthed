# September seed, draft one

Candidate `cultural_events` rows for September, researched 8 September 2026.
Nothing here is in the database. This is for review.

Every row was checked against the page it cites, and the "source says" line is
what that page actually states. Rows that could not be verified were thrown
away rather than softened, and the rejection list at the bottom is the more
useful half of this document.

## What this is not

The first pass at this suggested Nevermind, Friends premiering, Lehman
Brothers, Hendrix, Nintendo being founded and the invasion of Poland. Every
one of those fails rule one in `docs/internet-culture.md`: if the sentence
could sit on a Wikipedia date page without looking out of place, it does not
belong in this table. Those rows already arrive from the Wikipedia import.
What belongs here is internet culture and nothing else.

That test removed about two thirds of the obvious answers, which is the test
working.

## Coverage

Researched: September 2005 through 2019.
Not yet researched: September 2020 to 2025, and anything before 2005.

Ten days of the month have no row at all yet: the 2nd, 4th, 11th, 16th, 18th,
23rd, 27th, 28th, 29th and 30th. Some of that is the missing eras. Some of it
is real, and rule five says a thin day stays thin.

The 20th, 21st, 22nd and 26th are the densest days in the month.

### September 1

**2008 · The Google Chrome comic leaked** · tech · happened · high · not heavy
Google mailed out a Scott McCloud comic book explaining a web browser it had
not announced. One blogger got his copy early and put it online before Google
said anything.
Source: https://blogoscoped.com/archive/2008-09-01-n47.html
Source says: post dated 1 September 2008, "Today there was a comic book in my
mail, sent by Google and drawn by no less than Scott McCloud", and "I can't
see anything live there yet".

**2009 · Minecraft got monsters** · gaming · happened · high · not heavy
Survival Test added a health bar and things that wanted to hurt you. One of
them was the creeper.
Source: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition_Classic_0.24_SURVIVAL_TEST
Source says: "released on September 1, 2009, at 16:54 UTC", adding creepers,
pigs, skeletons and zombies, and a 20HP health system.

## Investigated and rejected

Kept because the reasons are the specification working, and because somebody
will suggest most of these again.

**Failed the Wikipedia date page test.** Google Chrome's actual launch, 2
September 2008, corporate product history. Microsoft buying Mojang, 15
September 2014, a 2.5 billion dollar acquisition. The Fail Mary, 24 September
2012, a football play. Nike and Kaepernick, 3 September 2018, an advertising
campaign and a boycott. "$#*! My Dad Says" premiering, 23 September 2010, a
television premiere. Sharpiegate, 4 September 2019, a political story about a
hurricane, and heavy twice over. Kanye West on the 2005 telethon, a televised
political moment during a hurricane.

**Could not be dated to a day.** It Gets Better, September 2010: the project's
own page says only "In September 2010", and Wikipedia's infobox and body
disagree with each other. Douyin's launch, 20 September 2016: one tertiary
source and no corroboration, on a date that would anchor the origin of TikTok.
Gamergate's September 2014 escalation: Wikipedia gives only "In September
2014" for the 4chan ban. The Emma You Are Next hoax: "late September" only.
The Renegade dance: month only. VSCO girls, "OK boomer", Tide Pods: circa
datings only.

**Wrong month, checked and confirmed.** Doge: nothing in September in any
year. Vine's shutdown announcement, 27 October 2016, and its last day, 17
January 2017. Snapchat's launch, July 2011. The Instagram egg, 4 January 2019.
The Sonic redesign, 2 May 2019. The Popeyes sandwich war, August and November
2019. Notch challenging Bethesda to a Quake 3 duel, 17 August 2011. The moth
lamp, July 2018. Among Us, released 15 June 2018. Star Wars Kid, Numa Numa,
Charlie Bit My Finger, Double Rainbow, Bed Intruder, Chocolate Rain, Evolution
of Dance: all elsewhere in the calendar.

**Real and dated, but no internet culture attached to the date.** Halo 3, 25
September 2007. Halo Reach, 14 September 2010. Grand Theft Auto V, 17
September 2013. Destiny, 9 September 2014, whose wizard came from the moon in
the June beta rather than at launch. Apple Ping and Google Instant, September
2010. Nintendo Switch Online, 18 September 2018.

**Left out deliberately.** PewDiePie's 10 September 2017 livestream slur:
datable and genuinely internet history, and a racism story with no upside as a
birthday row. Sarah Palin's Yahoo account, 16 September 2008: sourcing
conflicts between The Register and the popular retelling, and it reads as
election politics.
