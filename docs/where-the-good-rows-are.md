# Where the good rows are

Measured on 8 September 2026 against the live database, after the queue was
described as "some good, some mid".

---

## 1. The count that explains everything

| | Published | Candidate | Rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| September | 333 | 46 | 45 |
| Every other month combined | 12 | 0 | 3 |

345 published rows. 333 of them are September.

The site is not an almanac with thin patches. It is a September almanac with
eleven stubs attached, and no reader outside September has ever seen the thing
this project is for.

## 2. Why the queue feels mid

It is not the generator. Read what is actually waiting: Pizza Rat, Leave
Britney Alone, Don't tase me bro, Imma let you finish, Grumpy Cat's first
photo, Gritty, Bendgate, Storm Area 51, the Twitter onMouseOver worm, the
Chaos Computer Club beating Touch ID in two days. Those are not mid. Those are
the rows this site exists for.

What is mid is the tail, and there is a tail because September has been worked
to a depth of roughly 424 proposed rows. The good ones were published weeks
ago. What is left is the seventh best thing that happened on a September day,
which is a worse row than the seventh best thing that happened on any January
day, because nobody has taken the first six off January yet.

**The queue feels mid because the month is mined out, not because the bar is
wrong.** Running the generator harder at September produces more tail. The
first six things on 354 untouched dates are sitting there.

## 3. What the flags found, and what that proves

The panel now flags a row against the rules in `docs/internet-culture.md`.
Seven of the forty six waiting flag as thin, meaning under sixty characters of
context:

- Concord's servers went off. "The game had been on sale for two weeks."
- A Day Off Twitch. "Streamers went dark for a day over hate raids."
- Everyone started asking men about the Roman Empire. "The answer was more
  often than anyone expected."
- Unity announced it would charge per install.
- The day TikTok was due to be pulled from American app stores.
- Moo Deng filled a Thai zoo.
- OpenAI shipped a feed of AI video.

Every one is true, dated and sourced. Every one is a real thing people
remember. They are thin because nobody wrote them, and a row nobody wrote reads
as filler however good the underlying event was. Compare the same generator on
a day it was trying:

> Pizza Rat. A rat dragged a slice of pizza down the stairs at First Avenue
> station. Matt Little filmed it and posted it before lunch.

Same generator, same week. The difference is two sentences of writing.

So there are two separate problems and they were being felt as one. Some rows
should not be on the site. Some rows should be on the site and need rewriting.
The panel's Edit key already does the second in one keystroke, and it is the
more valuable of the two by a distance, because a rewritten Roman Empire row is
a better card than anything the generator will find on that date.

## 4. What filling the year costs

From `culture_search_runs`: a run covers one date, makes 3 to 9 searches, and
writes 3 to 6 rows. The forty dollar night gives the unit: 3,182 searches for
about forty dollars, so roughly 1.3 cents a search.

- 354 dates without a curated row
- about 6.5 searches a date
- **roughly 30 dollars, and about 1,400 rows to review**

That is an estimate in dollars before anything runs, which is the standing
policy after 6 September. It is one number and it should be checked against a
small batch before the whole year goes: run one week, measure the real spend
and the real keep rate, then decide.

The review load is the harder half. 1,400 rows at even ten seconds each is four
hours of somebody's attention, and the flags exist to make most of those ten
seconds unnecessary.

## 5. The order to do this in

1. **Clear the September tail.** Press f, work the seven flagged. Reject what
   should not be there, rewrite what should. An hour at most.
2. **Run one week of a fresh month** and measure. October or January, seven
   dates, and count what survives review. That number is what the thirty dollar
   estimate should be multiplied by, not a guess.
3. **Then fill the year**, in months, cheapest attention first.
4. **Lead lines after that**, not before. See `docs/lead-lines.md`. There is no
   point writing a card for a date that has one row on it.

## 6. The thing not to do

Do not point the generator at September again. It has been asked four ways and
it is answering with the seventh best thing on each day. The next amazing row
is not in that month.
