Hot 100, 1959 to 2025

The song at number one on the chart week covering September 7, every year the chart has existed. Tap a cover to open it. Pick a birth year and yours lights up.

How the ranking works

Four signals, none of them invented here. How many language editions of Wikipedia carry an article on the thing, which corrects for the fact that pageviews are in English and the calendar is not. How many people looked it up in the last sixty days, which is the closest thing to living memory that can be measured. Whether Wikipedia's own editors put it on their selected anniversaries list for this date. And whether any country still marks the date every year, which is the strongest signal there is and the reason Brazil's independence leads a page that also contains the launch of ESPN.

Gravity is a separate axis from rank, on purpose. A card about a killing does not get the same colour as a card about a television channel, however close their scores.
